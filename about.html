Our Heritage

Crafting Premium Photographic Memories

Born from a Passion for Flawless Visual Art

LUXEBOOTH was founded in 2018 with a simple realization: standard event photo booths were lacking. Low-resolution webcams, poor lighting, and slow, smudged printing were disrupting high-end weddings and corporate galas.

We set out to create a studio-quality setup that integrates beautifully into luxury event decors. Combining DSLR photography, custom-engineered lighting ring diffusers, and high-performance touchscreen software, we pioneered an interactive sharing platform that delivers portraits instantly.

Our Journey

Evolution of LUXEBOOTH

2018

Founding & First DSLR Setup

Launched in Beverly Hills with our first DSLR Open-Air booth. Redefined wedding photography standards locally.

2021

Interactive Mirror Innovation

Introduced full-length touchscreen mirror booths with premium software integrations and signatures drawing.

2024

Robotic 360 Video Launch

Expanded nationwide catalog with fully automated 360 slow-motion video setups featuring real-time render overlays.

Our Mission

To elevate event experiences through visual innovation. We combine advanced studio tech and software systems with warm, pro hospitality to ensure every guest walks away with a stunning, premium memory.

Our Vision

To remain the top choice for luxury event tech globally. We continuously research lens technologies, AI backdrop removal integrations, and thermal print layers to bring next-generation imaging to events.
