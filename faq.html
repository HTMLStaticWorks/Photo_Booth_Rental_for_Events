Detailed FAQ

Answers to Every Question

To reserve your date, check our online availability calendar, fill out the booking form, and make a 30% non-refundable deposit. Once received, our designers reach out to start graphic layout designs. Balance payment is charged 14 days prior to setup date.

Most booth setups require a clear flat space of at least 10ft x 10ft. The height should clear 8.5ft for standard backdrops. We require a dedicated wall outlet within 15ft of setup (running other lighting systems or bands on the same outlet causes fuse issues).

Absolutely. Custom vinyl wraps for the booth cabinet shell, custom touchscreen splash screens, and customized overlay templates can all be configured for corporate activations and branding events.

We cover the entire metro area. Travel up to 35 miles from our Beverly Hills office is fully free of charge. Travel outside this 35-mile radius is billed at $1.50 per mile (round trip).

We support classic 2x6 photo strips, larger 4x6 printouts, and custom metallic film prints. You can select single prints for guests or duplicates (one goes to guests, one goes into the guest leather album).

Cancellations made more than 30 days prior to event will be refunded everything except the initial 30% deposit. Within 30 days of event, payments are non-refundable. Rescheduling is free of charge up to 14 days before setup, subject to calendar availability.
